# Hue’s Touch

**Hue’s Touch** is a 3D platformer-collectathon developed by **Studio Infinite Loom**, led by **Miguel Torres**.  
This project served as the studio’s **first official collaboration** and was created for the **UCNJ Pitch Competition**, where teams present innovative projects for potential funding.  

---

## 🎮 Project Overview

Hue’s Touch was designed as a colorful and physics-driven 3D platformer prototype where players explore vibrant environments, collect items, and master movement mechanics.  
The project’s primary goal was to demonstrate the studio’s technical and creative potential while learning the fundamentals of **team-based development** and **game production pipelines**.

## 🚀 Key Features

- Custom **physics-based kinematic character controller** built from scratch  
- Prototype-level **3D platforming and collectible systems**  
- Collaborative workflow between programmers, designers, and artists  
- Early world-building and prototype environment design  

---

## 🏆 Competition Context

Developed for the **Union College of Union County (UCNJ) Pitch Competition**, Hue’s Touch represented **Studio Infinite Loom’s debut project**.  
While the project did not proceed beyond the competition stage, it demonstrated the team’s ability to conceptualize, plan, and deliver a playable product under time constraints.

---

## 🏗️ Status

**Status:** 💤 *Shelved*  
**Reason:** The project’s scope exceeded the studio’s available time and resources.  
Despite being discontinued, **Hue’s Touch** served as an invaluable learning experience — particularly for developing custom movement systems and understanding full-cycle production as a team.

---

## 🧠 Lessons Learned

- Building a custom character controller provided deep insight into **physics-based gameplay mechanics**.  
- Early-stage project management using ClickUp and Notion helped refine the team’s organizational workflow.  
- Understanding scope control and feature prioritization became crucial takeaways for future projects.
